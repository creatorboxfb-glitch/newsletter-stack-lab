Beehiiv vs Kit vs Substack: which fits your first newsletter?

There is no universal winner. Start with the job you need done: simple publishing, newsletter growth, or creator email workflows.

Substack

Consider it for a simple publishing and paid-subscription path. Substack says publication is free; paid subscriptions incur its 10% platform fee plus Stripe fees.

beehiiv

Consider it when newsletter growth and monetization features matter. The pricing page lists a free Launch tier up to 2,500 subscribers; paid prices vary with tier and billing.

Kit

Consider it for creator email workflows. Its Free plan lists up to 10,000 subscribers, forms, landing pages, broadcasts, and digital products; automations and sequences are paid features.

Decision rule: choose by the task you need to do in the next three months. Before paying or moving a list, verify current pricing, exports, data handling, payment availability, and upgrade triggers. These are feature-based starting points, not tested product rankings.

Official sources: Substack fees · beehiiv pricing · Kit Free plan.

Compare total costs: record the platform tier, payment fee, domain renewal, and any integration you actually need. Keep platform and processor fees separate; compare the same list size and billing interval.
